## Short description

A calm, precise PDF workspace. Add and edit text boxes, highlight, draw, and save. Everything stays on your device.

## Detailed description

Open a PDF and get straight to the finishing touches.

Paperline is a PDF viewer and annotation editor with a compact toolbar, a charcoal theme, and formatting that appears when you need it. Add a name, fill an empty area with a text box, highlight a passage, or sketch a quick note. Your documents are processed on your device.

EDIT WITHOUT THE CLUTTER
• Click to add text boxes. Drag, resize, or nudge them into place.
• Adjust text size, bold, italic, alignment, and colour.
• Highlight areas and draw directly on the page.
• Undo, redo, duplicate, and delete edits.
• Save a separate edited PDF. Reopen it in Paperline to keep editing your annotations.

YOUR PDF VIEWER, AUTOMATICALLY
• PDF links open in Paperline instead of Chrome’s built-in PDF viewer.
• PDF downloads can open in a new editor tab after they finish.
• Signed-in, same-site document links, including Moodle resource redirects and PDF embeds, keep their website login context.
• Turn automatic links and downloads on or off independently in settings.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
• No Paperline account, analytics, ads, or cloud conversion service.
• PDF rendering and exporting happen on your device.
• A local recovery draft helps you pick up where you left off.
• Online files are requested only from their original website. Existing website cookies may be included automatically by Chrome to load documents you are allowed to access.

GOOD TO KNOW
Paperline adds annotations over a PDF. It does not rewrite the original page text, perform OCR, redact content, or unlock password-protected files. Noto Sans is bundled for consistent text; some characters, including many emoji and CJK characters, are not supported for annotation export.

Site access is required to detect and open online PDFs automatically. To read downloaded files directly or open file:// PDFs, enable “Allow access to file URLs” in Chrome’s extension details. For protected resources, sign in to the original website first. Settings includes a setup check and an “Enable & repair automatic opening” button.
